隱私權政策

Torra · 最後更新：2026-11-24

這份政策適用於 Torra 的 App（Mac、Android、Windows）和網頁版。

Torra 存取哪些資料

  • 烘焙紀錄（溫度、風力、火力、里程碑、生豆資訊）和設定，存在你的裝置上。
  • 如果你登入 Google 帳號，Torra 只會使用 drive.file 權限：只能存取 Torra 自己在你 Google 雲端硬碟的「Torra」資料夾裡建立的檔案，看不到你雲端硬碟裡的其他檔案。
  • Torra 會讀取你的 Google 帳號 email，只用來顯示在畫面上，讓你知道登入的是哪個帳號。

怎麼使用這些資料

  • App：讓你的幾台裝置透過你自己的雲端硬碟同步烘焙紀錄和設定。
  • 網頁版：讀取已經同步的烘焙紀錄，顯示給你看。網頁版只會讀取，不會建立、修改或刪除雲端硬碟上的任何檔案。

資料存在哪裡、會分享給誰

  • 資料只存在你的裝置和你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。
  • 網頁版會把下載過的烘焙紀錄和 Google 給的存取權杖存在你這台裝置的瀏覽器裡，下次打開不用重新下載。登出就會全部清掉。
  • Torra 沒有自己的伺服器，不收集、不出售、也不分享任何資料給第三方，也沒有廣告或使用分析。
  • 網頁版顯示中文時，瀏覽器會向 Google Fonts 下載字型檔。這個請求不包含你的烘焙紀錄或帳號資料。
  • Torra 使用 Google API 取得的資料，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」的規定。

如何刪除資料、撤銷權限

  • 在 App 的「設定 → 裝置同步」登出，就會停止同步。在網頁版的帳號選單登出，會清掉瀏覽器裡存的紀錄和權杖。
  • 到 Google 帳號 → 第三方連結 移除 Torra，可以撤銷 Torra 存取雲端硬碟的權限。
  • 雲端硬碟上的資料可以直接刪除「Torra」資料夾；裝置上的資料可以在 App 裡刪除紀錄，或解除安裝 App。

Privacy Policy

Torra · Last updated: 2026-11-24

This policy covers the Torra apps (Mac, Android, Windows) and the Torra web viewer.

Data Torra accesses

  • Roast records (temperatures, fan and heat levels, milestones, bean details) and settings, stored on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google, Torra uses only the drive.file scope: it can access only the files it created itself in a "Torra" folder in your Google Drive, and cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Torra reads your Google account's email address only to show you which account is signed in.

How the data is used

  • The apps: to sync roast records and settings between your own devices through your own Google Drive.
  • The web viewer: to read the synced roast records and show them to you. It only reads; it never creates, changes or deletes a file in your Drive.

Where data is stored and who it is shared with

  • Your data stays on your devices and in your own Google Drive.
  • The web viewer keeps the roast records it has downloaded, and the access token Google issued, in the browser on your device, so they need not be downloaded again. Signing out clears all of it.
  • Torra has no server of its own. It does not collect, sell or share any data with third parties, and has no ads or analytics.
  • To display Chinese text, the web viewer has the browser download font files from Google Fonts. That request carries none of your roast records or account data.
  • Torra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Sign out under Settings → 裝置同步 (Device sync) in an app to stop syncing. Sign out from the web viewer's account menu to clear what the browser keeps.
  • Remove Torra under Google Account → Third-party connections to revoke its access to your Drive.
  • Delete the "Torra" folder in Google Drive to remove synced data; delete records in an app, or uninstall it, to remove data on your device.
